Goal — Defeat stress monsters along your adventure map. Each monster you beat earns XP and unlocks the next encounter. Reach Rainbow Peak to win!
Your moves — Breath moves (green border) open a guided breathing animation — follow it honestly, then tap "Done" to land the hit. Movement moves (purple border) require you to actually do the action (squat, walk, stretch) before clicking.
Combat — Each move deals Power damage to the monster. After your move, the monster strikes back for 5–16 random damage.
  • If your HP hits 0, you revive at 50 HP.
  • Deplete the monster's HP bar to defeat it.
Leveling up — Gain XP from every move. Level up when XP reaches 100 × your level. Monsters grow tougher with each encounter (Goblin → Wraith → Ogre → Dragon → loops).
Real-life rules — The game only works if you actually do the exercises! For breath moves, follow the animation honestly. For movement moves, complete the action before clicking. No cheating your own wellness!
