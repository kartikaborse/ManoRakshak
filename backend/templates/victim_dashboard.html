️ Court Hearing Preparation Guide

Step-by-step assistant guiding you through judicial procedures and protecting you from court strain.

Witness Trial Checklist:

  • Carry a valid government identity card (Aadhaar/Voter ID).
  • Carry a copy of the registered First Information Report (FIR).
  • You are entitled to travelling allowance and daily allowance from the court. Check in with the court clerk.
  • You can request an **in-camera hearing** (private court room) if you feel unsafe testifying in public.

Cross-Examination Survival Tips:

Defense attorneys may try to confuse or intimidate you. Remember:
1. Speak clearly, only answer the question asked.
2. If you do not remember, say "I do not remember". Do not guess.
3. You have the right to request a glass of water or ask the judge to repeat a question if it is unclear.

Free Legal representation (DLSA):

Under Section 15A of the SC/ST Act, you are entitled to free legal counsel. To request a court-appointed DLSA lawyer, contact the DLSA desk at the District Court or dial 15100.
